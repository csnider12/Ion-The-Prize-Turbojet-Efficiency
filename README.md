# Ion-The-Prize-Turbojet-Efficiency
2025 Ohio Academy of Science Project Submission

In my project, I set out to experiment with how utilizing a high voltage plasma to ionize intake air on a turbojet engine might impact its performance and longevity. Specifically, I asked how factors such as power output, internal temperature, and exhaust gases may be affected. In addition to these, I analyzed the jet after testing to determine operational efficiency and any other potential problems such as accelerated corrosion.
